# Academic Hub - Professional Academic Services Website

A modern, responsive, and elegant website for **Academic Hub**, an academic writing, thesis consultation, data analysis, and research support platform based in Islamabad, Pakistan.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Multi-Page Architecture**:
  - `index.html`: Modern landing page with interactive canvas particle animations, stats counter, and service overviews.
  - `services.html`: Comprehensive breakdown of 8 academic service offerings (complete thesis & dissertation writing, research papers & journal articles, assignments & essays, systematic literature reviews, research proposals & synopsis, empirical data analysis, academic proofreading & formatting, and defense presentation/viva preparation).
  - `how-it-works.html`: Clear 4-step workflow explaining the consultation and delivery process.
  - `reviews.html`: Testimonials from university students and researchers across Pakistan.
  - `about.html`: Team overview highlighting collaborative multi-disciplinary academic working groups.
  - `contact.html`: Project inquiry and quote consultation form with email integration.
- **Design & Aesthetics**:
  - Custom Dark Charcoal & Slate color scheme with refined golden yellow accents.
  - Interactive canvas particle background effects.
  - Scroll-triggered animations (`data-aos`) and number counters.
  - Mobile-responsive navigation menu with hamburger toggle.
  - Floating direct-action email consultation button.
- **Privacy & Confidentiality**:
  - Dedicated client consultation flow directing inquiries to `hoceinsadaqat005@gmail.com`.
