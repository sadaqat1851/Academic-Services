Frequently Asked Questions

⏱️ What are the typical turnaround timelines? +
Turnaround times depend on your project scope and depth. A complete thesis (Chapters 1–5) typically spans 10–21 days with incremental milestone reviews. Individual papers and chapters take 5–10 days. Data analysis and technical coding can often be accomplished in 2–5 days. Accelerated timelines are accommodated when feasible.
🔄 How do revisions and supervisor feedback work? +
We provide dedicated revisions based on your supervisor's remarks. Once you receive your draft, you can compile any requested modifications, and our team will refine and update the content accordingly.
🔒 Is my research data and project confidential? +
Yes, confidentiality is strictly guaranteed. Your identity, university affiliation, datasets, and project files are never published, shared, or redistributed to third parties.
🎓 Which universities and subjects do you cater to? +
We assist scholars across all leading Pakistani institutions including NUST, COMSATS, SZABIST, QAU, FAST, Bahria University, Air University, and many more. Our multidisciplinary group covers Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Finance, Social Sciences, Management, and Biotechnology.
📑 How can I provide university formatting guidelines or templates? +
You can email your institution's thesis guidelines, margin specifications, or formatting templates along with your inquiry to hoceinsadaqat005@gmail.com, and our editorial desk will format the document accordingly.
